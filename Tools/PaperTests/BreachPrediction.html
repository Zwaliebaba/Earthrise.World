How this works

You will see 16 damaged sections of a castle's stone wall, one at a time. Each is 8 m long, 2 m thick and 7 m high. Each stands on earth between two undamaged sections of the same wall.

The pictures

  • Front: the section seen from outside the castle, as if cut along its length. Plain stone is the outer face. Darker, striped stone means the outer face is gone there and only the inner face is left. Pale blue means you can see right through. The top 2 m are battlements, and their gaps are built that way, not damage. Under the wall is the ground it stands on, and black there is a gap in the ground.
  • Top: looking down, with outside at the bottom. The numbers are heights in metres above the ground around the wall. A minus number is a trench.
  • The dark panel beside them is the integrity view, the game's reading of this section.

The question

No more shots will be fired. Anything that is going to fall falls, and the dust settles. Is the section then open or shut?

Open means foot soldiers can walk through this section, from the ground outside to the ground inside.

  • A soldier is 2 m tall and needs a gap 1 m wide and 2 m high.
  • Soldiers stand on ground and on rubble. They never stand on the wall's stone, not even on a low ledge.
  • They climb up or down 1 m in each 1 m step.
  • Fallen stone becomes rubble. Rubble settles into a slope soldiers can walk on, and it never stays on top of standing wall.

Give your best answer, and say in a few words why. There is no time limit, and you cannot go back to an earlier item.
