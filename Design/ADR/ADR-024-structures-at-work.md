# ADR-024 — Structures at work: the built mask, critical parts, repair and its lock, gates and garrisons

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-02, by order M4-D1 ([MvpPlan-M4.md](../MvpPlan-M4.md)), which gives each structure its built mask, its critical part's share and whether it works; decision 7 landed with M4-B3, decision 6's gate with D2, decision 5 with D3, decision 6's passage attack with D4, and decisions 3 and 4 with D5, but for the lock from trench cells, which landed with D6, which digs them ([ADR-029](ADR-029-trenches-and-undermining.md)). What the code fixed that decisions 1 and 2 left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-D1):
- **The record at version 2 holds every field the plan's contract gives a structure but its garrison:** the mask, the share, working, the lock, repair's progress, the gate's flag and the control's hit points, the later orders' fields saved from the start. The garrison stays where M4-B4 put it, on each squad as the building it is in, so that it has one owner.
- **The mask is a bit a template voxel,** in the template's order, x fastest, then z, then y, bit i in byte i / 8 from its lowest; an empty template voxel's bit is clear.
- **The share is floored to a whole percent,** and is 0 for a critical part with no built voxel, which only construction (M6) leaves. A structure is working when it is at least the template's threshold from `STRC`, and always when its template has no critical part or no `STRC` record.
- **A structure is touched** in a tick when its box holds one of the tick's edits, those made between ticks included, or a collapse of its starts or ends; every structure whose box holds an edit is touched, where two boxes overlap. Step 10 recomputes only those, so a quiet tick costs nothing.
- **Working changing raises `StructureStopped` or `StructureWorks`,** its amount the share and its voxel the structure's origin, on the tick it changes.

Decision 6's gate landed with order M4-D2 (2026-11-02). What the code fixed that it left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-D2):
- **A gate is a structure whose template's critical part is named Gate,** T5 and T7, and its voxels are that part's, at their turned places.
- **`SetGate` is refused** `Invalid` for a structure with no gate, `NotOwned` for another player's, and `NotWorking` to open one that does not work; shutting is never refused, and a `SetGate` that changes nothing does nothing.
- **Navigation reads every open gate's voxels as air** when it rebuilds a cell, the set replaced each time a gate swings. A gate opened or shut in step 1 has its tiles rebuilt in that tick's step 8 with the edits, and the squads short of their goals or crossing those tiles search again, as after an edit. A gate that stops working is shut in step 10, after step 8, so its tiles are rebuilt there and then.
- **A load reads the open gates' voxels as air again** from the structures' records, since navigation is rebuilt rather than saved (ADR-014, decision 4).

Decision 5 landed with order M4-D3 (2026-11-02). What the code fixed that it left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-D3):
- **A squad on its way to garrison** is one ordered to Move with the building as its target, so the record needs no field of its own; it goes in, in step 3 before targets are taken, once its lead stands within a cell of the footprint and no longer in a step. It walks to the cell nearest the footprint's middle that it can reach. If by then the building does not work or has no room, it stops there.
- **`GarrisonSquad` is refused** `Invalid` for a building with no room at all in `STRC`, or for an engine's body; `NotOwned`, `NotWorking`, and `Full` when the squad's figures do not all fit.
- **A garrison looses from the cell just outside the face of the building's box nearest its target,** at the stand height above the box's base, its elevation and its reach counted from there. Each ranged figure inside takes, when its reload is due, the nearest enemy it reaches with nothing solid on the line, ties to the lower id, in step 4 after the rams. **Amended by M4-F2** (2026-11-02): its owner may aim a garrison at another player's squad or engine with `AttackTarget`, and its figures then take the nearest of that squad's figures they reach, falling back to the nearest enemy while none is in reach; it is how the Siege defender chooses its targets (ADR-027).
- **Healing runs on the tick's count:** every garrisoned figure gains a hit point, up to its kind's, on each tick whose count is a multiple of `garrisonHealTicksPerHitPoint`, in step 2.
- **Put out** means the nearest cells foot can use, ring by ring about the box, each ring in index order, on the owner's side first and then the rest, and none in a falling piece's strip. The owner's side is the side of the footprint's centre line that holds the owner's keep: the trial's keep, or else the owner's first T2. The line runs along the footprint's longer side, so a wall's lies between its faces, and a square footprint's runs across the keep's larger offset. Without a keep, and for the keep itself, every side is the owner's. (M4-D3 had it as the half-plane through the centre facing the keep, which tilts with a keep off to one side; M4-F1 corrected it to decision 3's centre line, 2026-11-02.)

Decision 6's passage attack landed with order M4-D4 (2026-11-02). What the code fixed that it left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-D4):
- **The passage is the template's empty voxels joined face to face to its gate's,** at their turned places. With T5's gate at the passage's inner end, that is the 3 by 6 by 4 m before the gate, and nothing behind it. A unit is in the passage when a cell of its footprint is, at the height it stands at, so a ram is in it with one of its nine cells inside.
- **The attack runs on the tick's count:** on each tick whose count is a multiple of `passageAttackTicks`, in step 4 after the garrisons' arrows. A unit that walks in takes its first hit from 0 to 4 s later.
- **Every unit means every unit,** as input 13 says: the defender's own figures in the passage are struck too, so a sally that stands there pays for it. Garrisoned figures stand in no cell and are not. The owner kept it on 2026-11-02 (GameDesign.md, *M4's phase D*, item 4).
- **It stops with working.** A gate below T5's 50 % stops the gatehouse in step 10, which ends the attack and puts the garrison out in the same step.
- **Measured by `PassageAttackTests`** (the suite built with clang on Linux, 2026-11-02): a ram at T5's gate, ordered at the gatehouse, strikes on its first tick and takes four of the gate's twelve voxels a blow. T5 stops working at its second blow, 15 s after the first, with four voxels left, 33 %. The third blow opens the passage at 30 s. The ram loses 30 of its 300 hit points to the passage, three hits. Against GameDescription.md's four minutes, the gate falls sixteen times sooner, and the passage attack costs the ram a tenth of its hit points. The owner took the smaller crater in timber, 1 voxel (GameDesign.md, *M4's phase D*, item 3), and with it measured: T5 stops working at the seventh blow, 90 s after the first, and opens at 165 s, the ram losing 220 of its 300 (ADR-023, after M4-D4).

Decisions 3 and 4 landed with order M4-D5 (2026-11-02). What the code fixed that they left unsaid, and the one rule it changed, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-D5):
- **`RepairStructure` is refused** `Invalid` for a squad that is not a laborer's, for a structure the world does not hold, and when no cell to repair it from is open to foot; `NotOwned` for another player's laborer or structure. The laborer walks to the cell nearest it, within `repairReachVoxels` of the footprint, outside it and on the owner's side, that foot can use. While set to repair it takes no target, as a moving squad takes none, and strikes only what touches it. One whose structure is gone turns idle.
- **Repair runs in step 2, after the garrisons heal.** A locked structure counts its lock down and gains nothing. One unlocked, with a laborer of its owner's set to repair it standing within reach of its footprint on the owner's side, gains `repairCubicMetersPerTick` while it has a voxel to put back and its owner a cubic metre of that voxel's material; at a whole cubic metre the voxel goes back as an edit and is paid for, and the progress over it carries on. A second laborer adds nothing.
- **A voxel to put back** is a built voxel whose cell is empty and holds no unit's footprint up to its headroom. It must stand on what is below it or, where the template has nothing below it, as under an arch, beside something solid across its layer. The support search judges it again once it is back (ADR-004).
- **The order is changed: lowest first, then nearest the footprint's centre, then a column through the template's depth at a time, along its x, each from its front,** which on a wall is its outer face, in place of decision 3's "then by template order". The template's order puts a wall's inner face back first. A lobbed shot, descending at 45°, cannot reach an inner voxel behind an open outer one; it strikes the base below instead, and lowest first puts that back before anything else. Measured on the breach run's W2 by `RepairTests` (the suite built with clang on Linux, 2026-11-02), with one trebuchet at 20 s and a laborer 3 m inside: unrepaired, 5 shots and 1,649 ticks from the first shot to the slot's fall; repaired in the changed order, 8 shots and 2,849 ticks, **1.73×**, inside GameDesign.md's Decided 1.3–2× (*the paper tests' values*, item 3) and near the plan's 1.7× by arithmetic. The attacker there shoots, at each reload, a column of the hole whose two voxels stand, fresh or put back whole, then one whose outer voxel stands, then any. By variants of that run not kept in the suite: the template's order gives 2.9× with that attacker, and 5.6× with one that shoots the first column west to east with anything standing; the changed order gives 3.7× with that one, since it spends a whole shot on each voxel put back. The voxel crater's grain makes repair stronger than the route table's model wherever the attacker takes back single voxels, so the Siege attacker (ADR-027) shoots fresh or whole columns first. The owner confirmed the change on 2026-11-02 (GameDesign.md, *M4's phase D*, item 2).
- **The material is the voxel's own:** timber for a voxel whose behaviour is timber, stone for the rest. A player the match has no record of pays nothing, as for a shot. Each voxel paid for raises `StoneSpent` or `TimberSpent`, and the trebuchet's stone now raises `StoneSpent` too, so the trial's log has every spending (ADR-026, decision 5).
- **The lock:** a stone whose strike or crater has a voxel in a structure's box locks it, and a ram's blow locks the structure it strikes, each for `repairLockTicks` from that tick; repair waits out exactly those ticks. `IntegrityReading` carries the lock, at query record version 4.

It was proposed, 2026-11-01, with the M4 plan ([MvpPlan.md](../MvpPlan.md), M4; [MvpPlan-M4.md](../MvpPlan-M4.md)). M3 makes structures entities with a template, an origin, a turn and a name (MvpPlan-M3.md, M3-B1). This records what a structure does in M4: whether it works, how it is repaired, how a gate opens and how a garrison fights. The numbers are the owner's inputs (MvpPlan.md, M4, inputs 10 to 13). The owner gave it the go-ahead on 2026-11-01, as written (MvpPlan.md, M4, input 1; GameDesign.md, *M4's values*, item 1). Order M4-D1, which first implements it, marks it Accepted (AGENTS.md §6). **Decision 7's section landed with order M4-B3** (2026-11-02): `STRC` and `Simulation::TemplateRules`, for T1 to T7, the threshold 0 for a template that names no critical part, and every template's control hit points at input 19's first value of 900 until M4-F5 calibrates them; the decision stays Proposed until D1.

## Context

- **The design's rules** (GameDesign.md, *Where RTS logic meets destruction*; GameDescription.md, *Templates* and *Fortifications*):
  - a building stops working when a critical part falls below its threshold, the share of the part's voxels still standing and supported, and is a ruin until repaired;
  - repair puts back voxels once built and later removed, pays for their material, works from the owner's side at a fixed rate per section however many laborers are on it, and not on a section hit in the last ten seconds by an engine shot, a ram blow or a trench;
  - towers and keeps garrison and fire; garrisoned ranged units fire from height, their shots start at the outer face of their tower, and they heal slowly; when a tower stops working its garrison is put out at its foot, on its owner's side and clear of where it would fall;
  - the gatehouse opens for its owner, its timber gate is its weak point, and its garrison fires down into the passage in front of the gate with crush damage a ram's roof does not stop.
- **[ADR-005](ADR-005-content-formats.md), decision 10:** a saved structure holds its template, origin, turn, name and a mask of which voxels have been built. M3's record is version 1 without the mask.
- **The templates mark their parts** (ADR-005; `ServerTemplate::parts`): critical, foundation and stock, and each critical part's name: T2's great tower, T4's firing platform, T5's and T7's gate. Nothing reads them yet.
- **The v3 asset package decided the gate's form** (GameDesign.md, *the MVP assets v3 package*, item 3): the gate's voxels stay in the world as T5's and T7's critical part, so cost, damage and craters are unchanged; open or shut is a flag on the instance; while open the owner's units pass and shots still stop; the client draws G1 or G2 in place of its voxels.
- **The route table's values** ([Review/PaperTests/SiegeRouteTable.md](../Review/PaperTests/SiegeRouteTable.md)), Proposed: repair at 0.08 m³ a second per section, a gate working while half its 12 voxels stand, six archers a garrison, reach 40 m from the outer face at two steps.

## Decision

1. **A structure records which of its voxels were built,** as ADR-005, decision 10, says: the record rises to version 2 with the mask, one bit per template voxel in the template's own order. A scenario's structures start fully built. A template voxel is **standing** when the world holds, at its turned position, the index the template puts there and no collapse in warning or falling holds it.
2. **Working is read from the critical part.** A structure with a critical part works while the share of the part's built voxels that stand is at least its threshold, in whole percent, from the `STRC` section (decision 7). The share is kept per structure and updated in step 10 from the tick's edits and from any collapse holding its voxels that started or ended in the tick, so it costs nothing on a quiet tick. A structure that stops working raises an event; its garrison is put out (decision 5), and a gate that stops working is shut for good until repaired above its threshold.
3. **Repair is a job of the owner's laborers, at a fixed rate per section.** A structure is repaired while at least one of its owner's laborers, ordered to repair it, stands within 3 cells of its footprint on its owner's side: the side of the footprint's centre line nearer the owner's keep. It gains `repairCubicMetersPerSecond` of progress a tick, as Q16.16 a tick; each whole cubic metre restores one removed built voxel, lowest first, then nearest the footprint's centre, then by template order, into an empty cell only, and takes one cubic metre of the voxel's material from the owner's stockpile. A voxel that would stand unsupported is skipped until one below it is back. Rubble in a voxel's cell stops that voxel; clearing rubble is a designation, which M4 does not build.
4. **The lock.** An engine shot whose strike or crater touches a structure, a ram blow on it, or a trench cell dug within 2 cells of its footprint sets its `repairLockTicks` to 10 s of ticks; repair waits while it is above 0, and it counts down in step 2.
5. **A garrison is units inside a building.** `Garrison` moves a squad into a building of its owner that works and has room, when its lead reaches the building's footprint; garrisoned units occupy no cell, are invisible to everyone but their owner, take no damage, heal one hit point every `garrisonHealSecondsPerHitPoint`, and fire if ranged, from the point of the building's outer face nearest the target, at the building's stand height, so their elevation steps count from there (ADR-023, decision 5). `Eject`, or the building stopping working, puts them out on the nearest cells on the owner's side clear of the building's footprint and its fall strip, in unit-id order.
6. **The gate is a flag, and the passage attack is the gatehouse's.**
   - **Open:** `SetGate` by its owner opens or shuts a working gate. While it is open, navigation reads the gate's voxels as absent, for every unit (MvpPlan.md, M4, input 13): a sally can be followed in, and the defender's AI shuts it when an enemy comes near. Shots and craters still treat the voxels as solid; the client draws G1 or G2 in their place.
   - **The passage attack:** while a gatehouse works and holds at least one garrisoned unit, every `passageAttackSeconds` it deals `passageAttackDamage` of crush to every unit, the ram included, in the passage's cells outside the gate. The ram's roof is pierce armor and does not stop crush.
7. **Per-template values are a server-data section, `STRC`,** baked from `GameData/Server/Structures.json`: each template's critical threshold, garrison capacity, stand height, its vision bare and garrisoned, and its hit points in the control ([ADR-028](ADR-028-the-control.md)). The templates' own files stay shared content; these values are the server's to tune (ADR-008).

## Consequences for the code

- **`Simulation::Structure`** gains the mask, the part's share, working, the lock, the gate's flag and the garrison, saved and hashed at record version 2; **`StructureView`** carries working, the share, the lock, the gate and the garrison's count.
- **Navigation** reads an open gate's cells as air when it rebuilds them, and `SetGate` marks those tiles for step 8, as an edit would.
- **Step 2** gains repair and the lock's countdown; **step 4** the garrisons' arrows and the passage attack; **step 10** the parts' shares and their events.
- **The integrity reading** (MvpPlan-M3.md, M3-B5) gains the lock's ticks, so the view shows "Locked · n s" (GameDescription.md, *Readability*).

## What this forecloses

- Repair that adds voxels never built, builds outside a template, or runs faster with more laborers.
- Garrisoned units that can be hit, or a garrison that fires from inside the footprint.
- A gate that changes the world grid when it opens.

## Left open

- **Construction,** M6's, which fills the mask from empty.
- **Clearing rubble,** M6's designation, which a breach's repair waits on.
- **Each template's threshold,** first values in MvpPlan.md, M4, input 10, for M5 to tune.
- **Whether only the owner's units should pass an open gate.** That needs navigation per owner, which ADR-015 does not have; input 13 records the choice.
