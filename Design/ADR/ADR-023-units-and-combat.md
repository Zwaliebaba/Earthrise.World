# ADR-023 — Units and combat: statistics as server data, damage as integers, arrows as physical shots

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-02, by order M4-C1 ([MvpPlan-M4.md](../MvpPlan-M4.md)), which gives units their hit points, turns collapses' and falls' crush into damage, removes the dead, gives each engine its body, and refuses the commands later orders build; decisions 3 and 4 landed with C3, 5 and 6 with C4, and 7, 9 and 10 land with C5 and C6. What the code fixed that the decisions left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-C1):
- **Crush beyond a weapon is taken less the target's armor against crush, at least 1,** as decision 2's formula takes it, with no bonus and no elevation. Every first value's crush armor is 0.
- **An engine takes its id before its body,** whose squad and unit take the two after it; the engine stands where its body stands, after steps 3 and 9, and has its body's hit points. A MoveSquad for a body is refused `NotPacked` while its engine is not packed.
- **The update is built for each player of the match,** and for player 1 in a world with none, as M3's was: it names that player's refused commands and that player's stockpiles alone. M4-E1 filters the rest of it ([ADR-025](ADR-025-fog-of-war.md)).
- **A refused command's reasons** are `NotOwned` for another player's squad or engine, `Reloading`, `NotPacked`, and `Invalid` for one that names nothing, aims out of range, or that a later order builds.

Decisions 3 and 4 landed with order M4-C3 (2026-11-02), with melee and the squads' orders. What the code fixed that they left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-C3):
- **Targets are chosen at the start of step 3,** before the searches and movement, from where every unit stands: each figure with an attack takes the nearest enemy figure within its vision, by squared distance across the ground from cell to cell, ties to the lower id. A squad attacking takes the nearest figure of its target, a squad or an engine's body, at any range. A moving squad's figures take none: they strike whatever touches them and walk on.
- **A figure closes on its target** by ADR-022's greedy steps while those reach it within its vision, and stands once the two touch; otherwise it keeps its place in its squad's walk. A holding squad never steps. A figure that waits while it has a target is waiting on purpose, and its wait does not count towards a search.
- **Two units touch** when their footprints are at most a cell apart across x and across z, a machine's square counted whole; height is not compared.
- **A reload counts down at the start of each tick's melee,** so a figure strikes on the tick it first touches an enemy and every `reloadTicks` after, 40 ticks for every soldier.
- **Orders end:** a Move or a Storm turns Idle when its lead stands at its trail's end; an Attack turns Idle where it stands when its target is gone, and its goal follows the target's lead, searching again when that lead has gone 4 cells from it. A storming or attacking squad whose lead left its trail to fight searches again from where the lead stands once the lead has no target. Stop and Hold end the walk where the figures stand, each finishing the step it is in.
- **Split and merge.** Split gives the highest-id figure a squad of one, idle, facing as its squad did, and is refused `Invalid` for a squad of one. Merge fills the first squad up to its kind's size from the others in turn, lowest ids first; a squad left with none goes, and the merged figures form about the lowest id, the lead. It is refused `Invalid` for squads of different kinds, an engine's body, a squad in a building, a squad named twice or fewer than two, and `Full` when the first is full already.
- **AttackTarget and StormTo are refused `Invalid`** for a squad with no attack on units; AttackTarget also for a target that names nothing, is the player's own, or is in a building. A ram's structure is M4-C6's.
- **The counters, measured** (`MeleeTests`' logs; input 6's arithmetic in brackets): six spearmen kill three outriders in 6 s and lose none, keeping 198 of 270 hit points [about 8 s, one or two lost], since each outrider strikes its own nearest spearman and their 72 hit points fall on three; three outriders kill six archers in 10 s, losing none [about 12 s; the archers' arrows are M4-C4's]; six men-at-arms kill six spearmen in 14 s and lose none, keeping 372 of 420. The owner judges whether outriders should do better against spearmen; it is a matter of input 6's numbers, not of the rule.

Decisions 5 and 6 landed with order M4-C4 (2026-11-02), with arrows. What the code fixed that they left unsaid, as amendments (2026-11-02, M4-C4):
- **A ranged figure also acquires what its reach covers** at the elevation to it, beyond its vision: an archer two steps up reaches 40 m and sees 32, and would otherwise never shoot as far as decision 5 lets it. Fog ([ADR-025](ADR-025-fog-of-war.md)) still limits what any figure may take.
- **The elevation's steps** are the whole 3 m in the height between the two cells' floors, towards zero, at most two either way, positive when the shooter stands higher. Its reach is 5 m more a step down and 5 m less a step up, never below 0, and the arrow's damage is decision 2's formula at those steps, fixed when it is loosed and carried by the shot, so a shooter that dies in its flight still hurts.
- **The line** runs from 1.5 m above the middle of the shooter's cell floor to 1 m above the middle of the target's, its length by an integer square root on Q16.16, its flight that length over 2 m a tick. What stops it, before loosing and in flight, is any voxel that is not empty, as the stone's shot counts it, water included. The hit compares cells across x and z alone.
- **When:** in step 3, after melee and in unit-id order, each figure with a ranged attack whose reload is due looses, the reload shared with melee's; in step 4 arrows fly with the stones, a new one its first 2 m on the tick it is loosed, and the dead are removed after the shots. An arrow is a `Shot` of kind `Arrow` whose `engine` is its shooter's squad, as `ShotView` says, with its shooter, target and damage; the shot's record is at version 2.
- **Decision 6:** a ranged figure with a target it cannot loose at closes on it by greedy steps within its vision, as melee's do; where those do not reach, it keeps its place in its squad's walk. An Attack order's walk is a path to its target, so the squad goes round what blocks it and stands where its line clears.
- **`SquadAim`** reads from the squad's lead's cell, its `targetVoxels` the cell a target stands in: OUT OF RANGE beyond the reach at the elevation, BLOCKED with the first solid voxel on the line, and IN RANGE otherwise, never TOO CLOSE. It costs a unit of query work, as the aim check does, and `QUERY_RECORD_VERSION` is 3.
- **An arrow is never re-aimed,** so a line clear when it is loosed meets no wall that was standing then. The plan's test of a target that steps behind a wall during the flight raises the wall across the line after loosing, as a gate shutting or a section falling would.

Proposed, 2026-11-01, with the M4 plan ([MvpPlan.md](../MvpPlan.md), M4; [MvpPlan-M4.md](../MvpPlan-M4.md)). It records how M4's units fight: where their statistics live, how damage is worked out, how arrows fly, and how engines, collapses and falls hurt units. The numbers are the owner's inputs (MvpPlan.md, M4, inputs 6 to 9), Proposed for tuning in M5. The owner gave it the go-ahead on 2026-11-01, as written (MvpPlan.md, M4, input 1; GameDesign.md, *M4's values*, item 1). Order M4-C1, which first implements it, marks it Accepted (AGENTS.md §6). **Decision 1's server data landed with order M4-B3** (2026-11-02): `UNIT`, `Simulation::UnitStats` and `Damage`, and `RULE`'s arrow, elevation, crush, ram and shot-price keys. The elevation's 15 % lives in decision 2's formula, `ELEVATION_STEP_PERCENT`, as the formula states it; the step's height, its band and the most steps are `RULE`'s.

## Context

- **The design fixes the model** (GameDesign.md, *Where RTS logic meets destruction*; GameDescription.md, *Combat model*): stat-based combat with hit points, attack, armor, range, speed and bonuses against classes; three damage types, melee, pierce and crush, with armor against each; shots that travel through the voxel grid and stop at the first solid thing; a shot passes units other than its target; arrows fly flat, engines lob; elevation in steps of 3 m, up to two, each +15 % damage and one band of range for arrows, and the same lost shooting uphill; engines can target engines; a unit whose line is blocked moves to where it can shoot.
- **The counters** are two linked cycles (GameDescription.md, *Roster*); the MVP has the Hall-era cycle and men-at-arms (GameDesign.md, *MVP*).
- **The simulation holds no float** ([ADR-013](ADR-013-ticks-numbers-and-randomness.md), decision 3); positions are Q16.16; durations are ticks.
- **Server data is tuned without recompiling the client** ([ADR-008](ADR-008-server-and-client-game-data.md)); [ADR-016](ADR-016-server-data-format.md), decision 3, names unit statistics as a section M4 adds.
- **M2 left three amounts to M4:** the collapse's crush damage, a drop's crush damage (GameDesign.md, *M2's values*, item 8; [ADR-015](ADR-015-navigation.md), decision 7), and the ram's craters ([ADR-006](ADR-006-voxel-sizes.md), *Left open*).
- **The arrow is drawn as a line model** ([ADR-018](ADR-018-line-models-and-the-line-pass.md)); the simulation sees a point.

## Decision

1. **Unit statistics are a server-data section, `UNIT`,** baked from `GameData/Server/Units.json`, one record per `UnitKind`: its navigation class, figures a squad, hit points, armor against each damage type, its attack's type, amount, reload in ticks and range in voxels (0 for melee), its bonus against each kind, its vision in voxels, its speed, and, for sappers and laborers, its digging rate. The bake converts each exactly, as `RULE`'s values are (ADR-016, decision 4). Siege engines' bodies are kinds of their own, `Ram` and `Trebuchet`, so that one table serves every unit. **Each value has one owner:** movement speeds move from `RULE`'s class keys to `UNIT`'s kinds, `RULE` keeping `footSpeedMetersPerSecond` for the collapse's warning alone (GameDesign.md, *M2's values*, item 4); the trebuchet's reload and range stay `RULE`'s shot keys and the ram's cadence `RULE`'s `ramBlowSeconds`, and their `UNIT` rows carry 0 there.
2. **Damage is one integer formula.** A hit deals `max(1, floor((attack + bonus) × (100 + 15 × steps) ÷ 100) − armor)`, where `armor` is the target's against the attack's type and `steps` is the elevation, −2 to 2, for pierce alone and 0 otherwise. A unit at 0 hit points dies at the point its damage is applied and is removed at the end of that step, in unit-id order; a squad with no figures left is removed with it.
3. **Melee reaches the eight neighbouring cells.** In step 3, after movement, every figure whose reload is due and whose target, or the nearest enemy figure in a neighbouring cell, is there strikes it. The tick's strikes are all worked out from the state before any of them land, then applied together, so the order of ids decides nothing about who strikes first.
4. **Squads choose their own targets inside their orders.** A squad that is idle, holding, storming or ordered to attack engages enemies within its kind's acquisition range, Proposed at its vision; each figure takes the nearest enemy figure, ties to the lower id. A holding squad strikes but does not leave its cells. An ATTACK order names a squad, an engine or, for the ram, a structure; STORM moves to a point engaging what it meets.
5. **An arrow is a physical shot.** A ranged figure whose reload is due looses at its target if the target is within its range plus a 5 m band for each elevation step, and the straight line from its loosing point, 1.5 m above its cell or, for a garrison, the building's outer face at the garrison's stand height (ADR-024), to a point 1 m above the target's cell meets no solid voxel. The arrow flies that line at `arrowSpeedMetersPerSecond`, Proposed at 40 (2 m a tick), traced voxel by voxel through the grid as the trebuchet's shot is; it stops at the first solid voxel and does nothing, or reaches its end and hits its target if the target stands within one cell of the point it was aimed at, and otherwise falls harmlessly. It passes every other unit. Bolts are crossbowmen's, which the MVP leaves out.
6. **A ranged squad whose line is blocked moves.** Ordered to shoot a target it cannot see a line to, it walks towards the target and tries again each tick, which is "the nearest place it can shoot from" at the cost of no search beyond its squad's path.
7. **Engines hurt units, and engines.** A trebuchet's shot deals its crush damage to every unit standing in the cell above the struck voxel or in its eight neighbours, and to an engine whose footprint holds the struck voxel's column. Firing at an engine is firing at its cell (Hud.md §8's attack cursor reads it as any target).
8. **Collapses and falls deal crush.** A collapse deals `collapseCrushDamage` to each unit it strikes, once (ADR-015, M2-F6); a drop deals `dropCrushDamagePerMeter` for each metre beyond 3. Both are in `RULE`.
9. **The ram strikes structures, at contact.** A ram ordered at a structure drives to the nearest cell from which its front touches it, and strikes every `ramBlowSeconds`, at the lowest standing voxel of the structure in the column before its centre, then the columns either side, alternately. A blow is a crater by the contact column of GameDescription.md's table, on the paper tests' ladder: timber 4 voxels, earth 1, and stone 1 when the voxel lies within the bottom metre of the structure and none above it. Its roof is its pierce armor; it has no attack on units.
10. **Shots cost stone,** `shotStoneCubicMeters` from the firing player's stockpile, and a `FireEngine` is refused below it. A refused command comes back to its player in the state update (ADR-020, amended).

## Consequences for the code

- **`ServerData`** gains `UNIT`, and `RULE` gains the arrow's speed, the band, the crush amounts, the ram's blow and craters and the shot's price; `Tools/CheckAssets.py` checks `Units.json` (every kind once, every bonus naming a kind, every value converting exactly).
- **`Simulation`** gains `UnitStats`, `Damage`, melee and targeting in step 3, arrows in step 4 beside the trebuchet's shot, the ram's blows in step 4, and hit points, reload and target on each unit, saved and hashed. Engines move as a squad of one unit, their body, whose kind is `Ram` or `Trebuchet`, while the engine's own record keeps its reload, its state and its facing.
- **`Protocol`** gains the new commands and the views' new fields (MvpPlan-M4.md, *Contracts*).
- **Tests** pin the formula, a counter's time to kill on each pairing of the roster, an arrow stopped by a wall, an arrow from 11 m reaching 40 m and one from the ground 30 m, a ram's blows on T5's gate and a T3's foot, and a shot's splash.

## What this forecloses

- Float, randomness or wall time in combat: a hit is decided by the rule, never by a roll.
- Arrows that pass walls or strike units other than their target.
- Soldiers changing the land: only the ram, engines, sappers, laborers, collapses and fire remove voxels (GameDesign.md).
- Statistics compiled into the code.

## Left open

- **Every number,** for M5's playtest to tune. MvpPlan.md, M4, inputs 6 to 9, are first values.
- **Hit chance and spread.** An arrow hits a target within a cell of its aim point, always.
- **Area attacks beyond the trebuchet's splash,** the mangonel's scatter shot among them, which the Trials leave out.
- **Where on the arrow the shot's point sits** for drawing (ADR-018, *Left open*).
