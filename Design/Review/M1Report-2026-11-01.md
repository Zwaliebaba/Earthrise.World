# M1 report: the renderer spike

**Status:** M1 closed by the owner, 2026-11-01, together with M0 ([GameDesign.md](../GameDesign.md), *Closing M0 and M1*). This report is MvpPlan.md's M1 task 13. Its figures were measured as stated below. None is an estimate.

## Verdict

**The 1 m baseline passes the pass mark with room to spare.** The voxel pass, its shadow passes included, takes 2.16 ms at the 95th percentile against 8 ms. The kill criterion does not fire, and [ADR-001](../ADR/ADR-001-direct-voxel-ray-casting.md) stands. The owner judged RendererLook.md's acceptance checks passed on screen, so question 4 calls for no lighting ADR.

## How it was measured

- **The machine is the benchmark** (owner, 2026-11-01): an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 Ti Laptop GPU on driver 32.0.16.1060, and an Intel Core i7-12700H. It replaces the desktop RTX 3060 that the pass mark was first written for.
- **The build is Release|x64,** from the branch that closes M1, built through `Earthrise.slnx`.
- **The GPU figures** come from `RendererSpike.exe --measure --windowed`, then the same with `--half-meter-world`. Each is one run of the owner's scripted path: round a 48 m square at the 84 m default view height, through all four rotations, 3,600 frames stepped at 1/60 s, with the first 60 left out as warm-up. The scene draws at 1920 × 1080 into a 1600 × 900 window and presents without waiting for the vertical blank. Quarrying runs at 94 voxels a second, the west curtain's northern section collapses at frame 600, and 111 units walk.
- **The CPU figures** come from `SimulationBench.exe`, which reports the median and the slowest of its repeated runs.
- **One run per configuration.** No run-to-run spread was measured.
